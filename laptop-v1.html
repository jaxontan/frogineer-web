01 · The problem

Most data lives in too many places.

Laptops, USB drives, an old server, a few cloud folders. Nobody is sure which copy is current, and nobody has tested a restore.

  • Duplicate and conflicting files
  • No clear owner or backup plan
  • One failed disk away from lost work
02 · Storage

One reliable home for every file.

A NAS sized for your team, with redundancy, hourly snapshots and backups that are tested, not assumed.

  • RAID that survives a failed disk
  • Hourly snapshots for instant rollback
  • 3-2-1 backups: local, off-site, cloud

NAS & storage · Synology, QNAP, TrueNAS · typically 1–3 weeks

03 · AI

Ask your files questions.

Private AI that runs on your own documents, so your team gets answers with sources instead of digging through folders.

  • Search across contracts, manuals and reports
  • Runs on-premises or in your own cloud
  • Every answer links to the source file

AI solutions · private search (RAG), assistants, automation · typically 2–6 weeks

04 · Cloud

Available anywhere. Recoverable always.

Your NAS syncs to the cloud, so every office and device sees the same files, and a fire or flood is an inconvenience, not a disaster.

  • Hybrid NAS-to-cloud sync
  • Disaster recovery with a written runbook
  • Cost and security reviews

Cloud · AWS, Azure, GCP · typically 1–4 weeks

05 · Interfaces

Dashboards your team will actually use.

I design and build the screens on top: monitoring, internal tools and websites, so the system stays healthy after I hand it over.

  • Health and capacity at a glance
  • Alerts before users notice problems
  • Clean, fast front-end builds

Web & interfaces · design and front-end · typically 2–5 weeks
